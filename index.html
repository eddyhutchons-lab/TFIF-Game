TFIF Game Prototype

Pick a character and steer them from the start to the finish flag. The stages never end: each one is a little longer and harder, and your best is the furthest stage you reach. Every fifth stage ends with a trophy and confetti. On a phone it plays full screen with touch buttons.

Turn your phone sideways to play

Keys

← →
Runner: walk backwards and forwards.
Flyer: slow down or speed up.
↑ Space
Runner: jump. Hold it for a higher jump. Land on beetles and bats to squash them.
Flyer: climb. Storm clouds hang over every tower, so fly through the gap between them.
↓
Runner: duck. Hold it and walk to crawl. On a shelf it drops you down through.
Flyer: dive.

Pause
