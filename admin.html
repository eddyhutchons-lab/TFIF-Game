How it looks in the game

Who can open this dashboard

Client: sees the reports, players, winners and downloads, and can't change anything. Operator: also sets the challenge, takes players out of the rankings, deletes players and gives access.

They sign in on this page with a code sent to that email. You can't change your own access here.

How the competition is scored

  1. Points: a finished stage scores stage number × 100, plus 10 per star, plus a speed bonus of up to 100 for finishing fast. An unfinished stage scores 10 per star.
  2. Stars: all stars collected in the period.
  3. Furthest stage: the highest stage cleared.
  4. Time played: minutes spent playing stages.
  5. Tiers (all-time points per game): Bronze, Silver from 1,500, Gold from 5,000, Platinum from 12,000, Diamond from 25,000.

Finding the winners

  1. Open Rankings and winners, choose the game, Last week or Last month, and what the challenge was won by. The top of the list is the winner, with their contact details.
  2. Download spreadsheet saves the list to open in Excel.

Fair play

  1. Every winner's plays are checked before they are announced. Stage times that are all very short, or a huge number of plays in a few minutes, can mean cheating.
  2. To take a player out of the rankings, click their row in Players and choose Take out of rankings. You can put them back the same way.
  3. To remove a player for good (for example when they ask for their details to be deleted), click their row and choose Delete player. This can't be undone.
  4. If a player asks for their details to be deleted, or you spot something odd, let the operator know and it will be handled.

Access

  1. To give a colleague access to this dashboard, ask the operator.
  2. Add or remove people in the Access tab. Give the client's people the Client role.
